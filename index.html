Portfolio Overview

The LAAA Team is proud to present the Wyman Dunford Portfolio — a 27-unit, four-property multifamily collection spanning North Hollywood and Van Nuys in the San Fernando Valley. The portfolio comprises assets built between 2005 and 2020, offering buyers a diversified mix of vintage, unit count, and regulatory profiles across two of the Valley's most active apartment submarkets.

Offered as individual assets rather than a package, the portfolio allows buyers to acquire one, several, or all four properties based on their investment criteria. Each property carries its own pricing, underwriting, and comp support. Three of the four properties sit within designated Opportunity Zones, contributing to increased neighborhood investment and development activity in these corridors.
